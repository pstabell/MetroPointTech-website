The short version

Every independent agency rents its operating system. Agency management from one vendor, CRM from another, accounting from a third. None of them talk, so agencies hire people to carry information between them and to do the work those systems won't — rekeying submissions, building certificates, checking policies, chasing renewals, answering the phone.

I didn't buy that stack, and I didn't bolt AI onto someone else's. I built the whole thing, and the AI runs it.

What's already built

Agency system
An autonomous agency management system. What an agency would otherwise run AMS360 for — except the servicing work runs itself.
Sales CRM
Pipeline and producer workflow. AgencyZoom's job, with the follow-up handled rather than assigned.
Accounting
A full QuickBooks-equivalent platform, bank feeds synced through Plaid, reconciliation automated.
Marketing
A maxed-out HubSpot-equivalent platform — campaigns, sequences, content — not a mailing-list tool.
Voice & email agents
They answer the phones. No hold queue and no missed call, however many come at once. They ask the follow-up questions a caller couldn't answer live, then email to request declaration pages and anything else still needed.

The AI isn't a feature on one screen. It's inside every part of this, running the operation — which is the only reason one agency can be built this way without a back office behind it.
